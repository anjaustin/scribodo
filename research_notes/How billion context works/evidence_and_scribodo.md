# Evidence, limitations and transferability to Scribodo

## 1. What do the scale, quality, cache and survival results establish?

### Takeaway

The useful achievement is long-lived functional memory through bounded working context, selective summaries and recoverable originals—not simultaneous attention to a billion unique tokens. The published evidence is encouraging but workload- and model-dependent; this research checked documents, arithmetic and test designs, not experimental outcomes.

### Cited Findings

**Scope and provenance.** All upstream code/document URLs below are pinned to `c3dfbe6b21a1b2fc3642594043bc81fd2ae91247`. That snapshot's package is **0.1.212**, embedded kernel **0.0.182**; its benchmark explicitly evaluates **0.1.202**, and the living paper labels its pilot “v0.1 numbers.” Do not attribute old measurements to the current code without a local replication. [Package][PKG]; [kernel package][KP]; [benchmark][B]; [paper §8.1][P]. Downloaded source copies are in `evidence_sources/` beside this note. No upstream code, tests, installers or model servers were run.

**Reported results — not independently reproduced:**

| Claim | What the reported number actually measures | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| “Billions” | Main host: 18.76B cumulative input = 1.75B fresh + 17.01B cache reads; about 24.7B across differing three-host observation windows. | Repeated prompt/cache reading counts repeatedly. Not unique retained archive size, active context, or successful recall. [Paper §§5–6][P] |
| Billion-token session | Leading OpenCode session: 12,049 calls, about 1.52B cumulative input, ~126K mean prompt; Pi: 8,584 calls, 1.01B cumulative. | Both use 1M-class model windows; the ≤204,800 boundedness observation comes from a different window class. [Paper §6.1][P] |
| Large archive | Main host's leading session has 6.45M tokens compressed; all session states total 229M compressed tokens. | Different unit/population from billions of cumulative request tokens. Production usage rows cover 917 of 4,843 session-state records. [Paper §§6.1,6.5][P] |
| “28.6B / 106 days” | Fitted single-session capacity simulation at a 1M window, cumulative-input accounting. | ~19× beyond the largest observed session; tier-1 uses tool-block p75 ratio 1/45, tier-2 fit uses 57 resolvable events, tier-3 ratio is unobserved/design-assumed. This is a forecast, not a run. [Paper §7][P] |
| ~70× fold | Heavy-session mean represented source ~910K versus roughly 13K resident summaries; paper describes the summary count/magnitude using medians. | Standing representation ratio, not 70× less end-to-end inference or 70× semantic fidelity. [Paper §§6.1–6.2][P] |
| 94.2% cache share | Cache-read tokens / (cache reads + fresh input), inter-call gaps <2 min, 89% of traffic; main blended 90.7%, ework 88.3%. | Token-weighted provider telemetry, not probability of answering correctly or local latency savings. README's healthy 95–97% guidance is a separate operational claim. [Paper §6.3][P]; [README][R] |
| 100% survival | BC completed the 199 reported 1.48M-token MAB recommendation instances where full-context requests returned context-length errors. | Means avoiding those request failures; quality is **18.58% Recall@10**, below BM25 22.08% and reranked retrieval 21.19%. Not all facts recalled, nor proof of durable recovery. [Benchmark §5][B] |

**Reported cross-benchmark quality, with fair benefits and failures:**

- LongMemEval-S, 500 instances, ~110K histories: BC 79.20% overall versus full 83.20% and best retrieval arm 62.60%. BC's multi-session reasoning 79.70% versus reranked retrieval 45.11% is evidence *for* retaining coherent conversational state rather than selecting isolated chunks alone. Its temporal score 70.68% still trails full 72.18%; knowledge update 88.46% trails full 89.74%; abstention 83.33% trails BM25 96.67%. “+16.6%” here is **16.6 percentage points**, not a relative percentage improvement. [Benchmark §§1,3][B]
- ContextBench, 500 instances over 58 repositories: BC utilized-span F1 0.3097 versus full 0.2928, evidence precision 0.3358 versus 0.3125, but evidence recall 0.3694 versus full 0.3712. This metric measures code-span selection/bug localization, not novel-writing quality or a general SWE task-resolution rate. Pruning intermediate tool noise is a plausible useful explanation; the aggregate comparison alone does not isolate which mechanism caused it. [Benchmark §4][B]
- MemoryAgentBench: BC 43.54% macro average versus reranked retrieval 55.21%, BM25 53.70%. At ~197K verbatim-needle QA, BC exact match **27%** versus full 69%, BM25 59%, reranked retrieval 66.33%; at ~180K BookSum, BC ROUGE-L F1 38.62% versus full 42.65%; at ~6K conflict resolution BC F1 89.97% versus full 92.80%. The report also attributes some needle failures to empty final `content` after answers in `reasoning_content`, so the exact-match drop is **not cleanly attributable to summary loss alone**. [Benchmark §5][B]
- “30×” in MAB corresponds approximately to 1.48M / **49.5K mean prompt**, not 1.48M / ~16K steady-state prompt (which would be ~92.5×). Preserve both figures and their denominators; README shorthand “30× steady-state” conflates them. [Benchmark §§1,5][B]; [README benchmark summary][R]
- The paper's controlled Qwen3.8-27B/sGLang/temperature-0 pilot reports 31–54% fewer billed tokens with tied/default repetitive-workload probes. However, sliding never exceeded its 65,536 window and threshold compaction never fired; these arms are effectively full retention at that workload point, not demonstrations of failure after truncation. No controlled run exercised tier-2+. [Paper §8.1][P]
- A zero-compression 128K control with doctrine+reference tags alone reproduced almost all the headline re-fetch improvement (62% versus 63%). Attribute the result to the bundle, not to autonomous compression decisions. On repetitive work fixed-cadence summarization was cheaper than BC default; on phase-structured work conservative BC had better probes than aggressive BC (81.6% versus 75.7%, with 1 versus 3 execution errors). Seed comparisons mix 32K/64K windows and per-arm probe availability differs. [Paper §8.1][P]
- Restoration succeeded 6/6 **when instructed**, but spontaneous adoption was zero for that 27B evaluation model. Repeated probes can leak answers back into history. At 16K, one-shot compaction was cheapest; the ~2K-token doctrine was reported as ~25% fixed per-call overhead in that workload. This matters more than the headline large-window savings for a small local model. [Paper §8.1][P]

**Independently checked methodology/artifact availability (not outcome validation):**

- The benchmark file calls itself an independent third-party evaluation, but the inspected report names no evaluator/organization, supplies no run dates/seeds, per-item predictions, judge prompts, chunking/top-k parameters, executable harness or confidence intervals. At this pinned tree the benchmark directory contains the English/Chinese reports and PNG charts. Thus report “**repository-hosted report describing itself as independent**,” not “independently verified benchmark.” [Pinned benchmark directory][BD]; [report][B]
- The report specifies models, shared prompt/judge/accounting policy and prices: DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017, Qwen3-Embedding-8B, Qwen3-Reranker-4B, Pi CLI 0.73.1. It cites Full Cost Attribution, but citing that methodology does not expose its own missing run artifacts. The referenced arXiv paper is about **Paritok**, not an independent validation of BC. [Benchmark §2][B]; [cost-attribution paper v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22114v1)
- LME's actual billed full-context cost is $0.42 versus BC $2.30 because shared haystacks give full 98.1% prefix reuse. The promoted $7.97 full versus $2.30 BC compares a modeled independent-session cost assumption. Both are useful scenarios; do not present 71.1% savings as the observed bill in that sequential run. MAB full cost excludes failed TTL and supplies a counterfactual surcharge; a crash-scored macro average mixes quality with availability. [Benchmark §§3,5][B]
- The paper itself states production stores, sanitized corpus, deployment extracts and experiment workspace/harness are **not yet public**. Figure-generation source is visible but reads a missing `figures/data/opencode-top-marathon.csv` and author-local paths; other plots embed aggregates/formulas. It is an inspectable plotting artifact, not a reproducible production-data release. [Paper Appendix A][P]; [figure script][FIG]; [pinned paper directory][PD]
- Production is predominantly one expert user's observational practice plus the autonomous ework service, not a randomized multi-user study. The paper explicitly says production telemetry cannot establish task quality; its [V]/[A]/[P] labels mean author-verified/analytical/planned, **not external verification**. Its broad “quality does not degrade” language must be read with its own limitations and controlled probe scope. [Paper §§5,8–9, Appendix B][P]

### Inferences

- **Interpretive conclusion:** A finite working window can support a much larger life history if selected information stays available and exact originals remain addressable. This meets the user's functional-memory objective in principle; billion-token instantaneous attention is not required. But “recoverable in storage” and “the model retrieves and uses the right evidence” are separate properties. [Paper §§3,8][P]
- **Retention warning:** §4.2 defines recoverable information, then uses ~1/8 text retention as a proxy and explicitly acknowledges text ratio is not semantic fidelity. The horizontal retention curve is an analytical assumption, not measured fact survival. Rare observed re-distillation does not prove constant semantic recall at arbitrarily long horizons; the architecture itself recursively summarizes blocks. [Paper §§3.2,4.2,7][P]; [figure 2 construction][FIG]
- **Not an argument against summaries:** summaries can preserve objectives, causal links and temporal relationships better than isolated top-k chunks. Exact names, quotations, revised dates, negations and speculative alternatives need source retrieval because compression may omit the only discriminating detail. Combining both is a testable response to the observed task split, not a guarantee. [Benchmark §§3–5][B]

### Gaps

- No independently rerunnable BC benchmark outcomes were verified in this assignment; the search chiefly returned upstream material and a secondary architecture article. Absence in this search is not proof that no external evaluation exists. Missing raw artifacts prevent checking sampling, grading, significance, costs and causal explanations. [Report][B]; [paper Appendix A][P]
- No controlled multi-tier semantic-drift curve, small-1.7B-model evidence, multilingual fiction-memory test, or project-authority/privacy evaluation is supplied by the inspected evidence. [Paper §§8–9][P]; [benchmark setup][B]

## 2. What constraints and failure evidence matter before reuse?

### Takeaway

The kernel provides bookkeeping, not validated summary truth or app-specific authority. Reuse also carries extra attribution language, JavaScript/runtime packaging, historical provenance claims and failure surfaces that should be evaluated rather than treated as solved by an MIT metadata field.

### Cited Findings

- **Source-verified license text:** top-level and kernel LICENSE each include standard MIT permission/warranty text **plus “Additional Term — Attribution on Product Surfaces.”** End-user-visible commercial/open-source products must visibly credit use on homepage, documentation, or About/Credits/Acknowledgements, including specified billion-context/acp-kernel links as applicable; purely embedded/server-side use can carry notices in shipped documentation. The kernel wording leads with acp-kernel, the proxy wording with billion-context. `license: MIT` alone omits material conditions. This is a description of the text, **not a legal opinion**. [Proxy license][L]; [kernel license][KL]; [package][PKG]
- **Provenance is author-reported:** `kernel/PROVENANCE.md` describes migration from opencode-acp, distinguishes original files from DCP-derived/AGPL expression, and says the latter's algorithms were rewritten in fresh expression or excluded. This is a useful declared provenance plan/audit, not independent proof of every file's origin or license compliance; this assignment did not perform donor-tree diff/legal review. Its older “genuinely MIT” description must still be read alongside today's extra attribution term. [Kernel provenance][PROV]; [kernel license][KL]
- **Source-verified runtime metadata:** full package declares Node >=20, direct dependencies mysql2 and zod, optional jimp; many build-time-listed libraries can be bundled, so that list is not a complete shipped-artifact dependency audit. Kernel declares Node >=20 and no production dependency section, but remains TypeScript/ESM compiled to JS, not a C ABI. README's container uses Node 24 and says its downloaded/builtin SQLite-engine path requires >=22. Select an actual pinned distribution/runtime path, not the lowest engine string alone. [Package][PKG]; [kernel package][KP]; [README Docker section][R]
- **Privacy/operations caveat:** README documents auto-update, a separate advisory checker which can force installation even when auto-update is off, and a remote-announcement feed. “Disable auto-update” is not evidence of zero outbound network behavior. A local deployment must separately constrain these channels and test offline/no-egress operation. No settings were changed during this research. [README update/advisory/announcement sections][R]
- **Host cost caveat:** README explicitly says compression reduces outgoing API requests, not the client's full conversation file, and cites tens of seconds per turn around 30K messages for one host. This is an upstream reported host-scaling problem, not a measured Scribodo bottleneck. [README FAQ][R]
- **Structural failure tests, read only:** issue847 tests guard repeated unsuccessful compression specifications, reversed ranges, sub-gate recommendations and circuit-breaking after three consecutive failures. These corroborate that model/tool orchestration can fail or loop; they do not test factual summary accuracy. [Issue847 regression source][T847]
- **Identity/isolation risk:** issue4110's regression documents degenerate fingerprints for assistant `content:null` tool-call turns leading to cross-session adoption; tests require tool-call-aware fingerprints and reject evidence-free turns. Treat as a source-documented historical failure with a regression at this snapshot—not an assertion that the current snapshot is still vulnerable. Stable explicit project/conversation identity is safer for Scribodo than reconstructing ownership from content similarity. [Issue4110 regression source][T4110]
- **“Drift” must not be misquoted:** `fold-drift.test.ts` tests changing/reserialized message IDs and preservation of fold coverage across projected utility requests, not repeated-summary semantic degradation. The summary-abort test checks deadline/client-cancel attribution and safe diagnostics, not truthful summaries. [Fold drift tests][TD]; [summary abort tests][TA]
- **Survival-test scope:** issue5631 runs a recording fake upstream with deterministic emergency digests, a ~6MiB raw fixture, repeated SIGTERM/SIGKILL/admin shutdowns and no real model tokens. It checks folded-body bounds, on-disk proof and >=50% byte-prefix stability. It is useful designed crash/fold regression coverage, but cannot establish provider cache-hit percentage, semantic recall or power-loss durability. It waits 500ms before kill to allow flushes; “any kill instant” would overstate it. Tests were not executed here. [Restart regression source][TR]

### Inferences

- Repeated-summary drift remains a real hypothesis to measure even with source lineage: an omission or false causal link in tier-1 can become more confident in tier-2. Recoverable originals permit correction only when the host/model knows what to fetch. Chronology requires distinguishing story time, conversation time and revision time; contradictions require distinguishing author correction, character belief and unresolved alternatives. These are proposed fiction-specific concerns grounded in the reported task-dependent losses, not upstream measured failure rates. [Benchmark §5][B]; [paper §§4,8–9][P]
- Logical validity (references exist; range coverage correct) is not semantic validity (summary accurately preserves facts and authority). No license, line-count, passing structural test or text compression ratio establishes the latter. [Kernel README][K]; [paper limitations][P]

### Gaps

- A deployment decision still needs a pinned shipped-artifact dependency/security review, license/provenance review appropriate to the chosen reuse, and an explicit offline configuration audit. No claim of full legal compliance or runtime compatibility is established here. [Licenses][L]; [kernel provenance][PROV]; [runtime package][PKG]

## 3. What should Scribodo adopt, and what experiment could establish fit?

### Takeaway

**Proposal, not implementation approval:** evaluate a bounded host-scheduled memory planner against retrieval-only before choosing an upstream dependency. The earlier native-first recommendation survives as a packaging/authority hypothesis, but upstream evidence does **not** establish that hierarchical summaries beat retrieval-only or simple compaction on LFM3-1.7B.

### Cited Findings

**Source-verified local baseline (working-tree snapshot, not remote-pinned source):** local HEAD is `3e7add862226e61abf34629bd973c3123b94ac18`; `MEMORY-001` and `PRODUCT-GAPS` were untracked planning documents at inspection. These local file citations describe the observed checkout and must not be represented as committed artifacts.

- Scribodo's product contract keeps MariaDB as structured authority and project manuscript files human-readable; Qdrant is a rebuildable derived index with source IDs/revisions/authority distinctions. Only explicit author actions edit text/establish canon. Observer findings enter creative chat only through **Discuss**, not merely because indexed/available. [SOW §§1,4–6](../../docs/SOW.md); [semantic API](../../src/llm/semantic.h)
- `chat_build_request_ex()` reserves 1,024 reply tokens +256 margin, gives attachments a shared budget up to 60% of the input allowance, and keeps newest fitting turns. The worker gets the actual selected per-slot context and copies all eligible prior message contents before bounded selection. Remote dispatch is author-selected; local-only turns are filtered out for remote requests. The path inspected contains no general historical-memory recall planner. [chat.c L221–402, L637–673](../../src/llm/chat.c)
- **Correction to a tempting cache assumption:** a code comment describes documents as stable-prefix context, but the implementation actually prepends attachments to the **newest user message** (L299–316,L373–380). Do not assume stable attachment-prefix reuse from the comment alone. [chat.c](../../src/llm/chat.c)
- Conversation loading queries all matching messages/relations in sequence without LIMIT and allocates the full result; the UI has per-message text/wrap caches and full-list height work. These are source-identified unbounded working-set candidates, **not measured performance defects**. [store_chats.c L183–262](../../src/app/store_chats.c); [chat_view.c L19–26,L114–170](../../src/ui/chat_view.c)
- Existing semantic API carries IDs, revision/range, kind/status; embedding is separate Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B, 1024 dimensions, with input text capped at 2000 bytes. Reusing this API is not proof that rare-detail recall or whole-book coverage is adequate. [semantic.h](../../src/llm/semantic.h)
- The local runtime report describes M4/16GB, LFM3-1.7B BF16, supervised llama-server via private authenticated Unix socket, `-fa off`, BF16 K/V, no host prompt cache, two slots, and memory-fitted effective context rather than guaranteed advertised 256K. It reports default 32K per slot, ~3.8GB mapped weights plus default server buffers (~1.4GB), separate embedder ~2.2GB, partial recurrent/sliding-window prompt reuse, and foreground stalls from unscheduled observer jobs. These are **existing local report measurements**, not rerun by this research. [MODEL-001](../../docs/MODEL-001-lfm3-runtime.md)
- GAP-003 requires whole-project latency/memory/backlog and soak evidence, not prompt compression alone; planning explicitly says investigation, implementation not authorized. [PRODUCT-GAPS](../../docs/PRODUCT-GAPS.md); [MEMORY-001](../../docs/MEMORY-001-long-lived-context.md)

### Inferences

**Proposal: concrete adoption decision matrix** (trade-offs inferred from the cited source boundaries, not benchmarked integration results). [Kernel API][K]; [proxy/runtime][R]; [local authority contract](../../docs/SOW.md); [local runtime](../../docs/MODEL-001-lfm3-runtime.md)

| Option | What can actually be reused | Scribodo-specific burden | Current decision / gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full proxy | Wire normalization, context tools, persistence/recovery and diagnostics | Node sidecar; Unix-socket/auth bridge and streaming/cancellation compatibility; duplicate archive/state; updater/advisory/announcement network policy; broad tool/prompt overhead; permissions and authorship isolation | **Do not adopt as default now.** Consider only a quarantined experimental arm if the narrower options cannot deliver needed behavior. No generic TLS interception is justified when Scribodo owns inference calls. |
| Kernel sidecar | Range/ref/block/tier lifecycle, rendering and compression policy primitives | JS process/IPC and versioned state; MariaDB remains authority; host must generate summaries, fetch originals, enforce eligibility, budget and schedule; extra attribution and packaging | **Credible comparison arm**, not a drop-in C library. Prefer if correctness/reuse saves enough engineering versus measured IPC/package/RAM costs. |
| Native conceptual equivalent | Design ideas: bounded recent zone, incremental derived briefs, source lineage, bounded exact restoration | C11 implementation/tests; own invalidation, job lifecycle, budgeting, search and UI explainability; must avoid unexamined copying and separately review any reused expression | **First hypothesis to evaluate**, not winner by definition. Host-scheduled first-tier briefs plus exact/semantic retrieval may suffice; add recursive tiers only if measured need/benefit. |

**Proposal: memory contract.** Keep four distinct things: authoritative original messages/revisions/canon; derived briefs tied to immutable source IDs/hashes/model+prompt versions; bounded source retrieval; a bounded rendered working prompt. A brief is neither canon nor permission to edit. Refresh from originals to test/limit drift; preserve original source times, story-time statements, correction/supersession and uncertainty. If a source is deleted, retracted, rescaled in scope or rendered ineligible, invalidate dependent briefs/ancestors. A stale summary job must not publish against a new revision. A top-k answer must not claim exhaustive book coverage. [SOW](../../docs/SOW.md); [MEMORY-001 hypothesis](../../docs/MEMORY-001-long-lived-context.md); [upstream lineage/restoration][P]

**Proposal: minimal discriminating protocol, before claiming model fit.**

1. **Freeze conditions and corpus.** Record C/app commit, model/GGUF hash, llama.cpp build and all BF16 flags, embedding hash, tokenizer/template, effective per-slot context, generation/output budget, seeds, ambient memory and cache state. Synthetic/private-free fixture: one representative novel-scale project (~100K words) and one accumulated project (~300K words), conversations at 100/1K/10K messages; a later stress stage can use 100K messages/1M words. Pin exact corpus bytes/tokens and ground truth. These sizes are **proposed workloads**, not upstream measurements or typical-user assertions. [Local GAP-003](../../docs/PRODUCT-GAPS.md); [MODEL-001](../../docs/MODEL-001-lfm3-runtime.md)
2. **Same-model arms.** A=current recent-window/attachment-cut baseline; B=A+bounded lexical/exact+existing semantic retrieval; C=B+host-scheduled first-tier briefs; D=C+hierarchical brief-of-brief promotion. Add E=matched single-summary compaction to check whether complexity is necessary. If comparing reuse, swap C/D's planner for pinned kernel sidecar with matched brief source/output budgets; full proxy is optional and separately costed. Use a source-supplied “oracle excerpt” control to separate inability to answer even with evidence from memory-selection failures. Do not give only one arm a better summarizer, extra context or repeated-probe answer leakage. [Paper's attribution/probe limitations][P]; [benchmark task split][B]
3. **Recall/quality tasks.** Predeclare at least a pilot set spanning exact quote/Unicode name/number, early rare fact, cross-session decision, timeline ordering, revised/retracted fact, unresolved contradiction, hypothetical brainstorming versus approved canon, and unanswerable question. Evaluate immediately and after 1/2/4/8/16 compressions; explicitly force tier promotion in D. Compare summary-of-summary against regeneration from originals. Use fresh one-shot probes/parallel branches so answers do not become their own memory. Report exact-match where warranted, source-supported answer correctness, citation/range accuracy, abstention, wrong-current-fact rate and author-rated useful continuity, separately by task—not only one macro score. [Paper pilot caveats][P]; [benchmark exact/temporal/conflict results][B]
4. **Instrument the whole bill.** Log actual rendered/tokenized prompt including doctrine/tools/ref tags, summaries and evidence, output reserve and safety margin. Count summary input/output/prefill/generation, rejected attempts/retries, query embedding, candidate retrieval, excerpt fetch, re-indexing and invalidation/rebuilds, and background backlog. Separate initial corpus ingestion from amortized per-turn and whole-session totals. Capture actual local reused/reprocessed tokens and wall time instead of applying provider cache-dollar discounts. A small outgoing prompt can hide more total local inference work. [Cost methodology caveat][B]; [local partial reuse](../../docs/MODEL-001-lfm3-runtime.md)
5. **Resource gates, proposed for agreement before tuning.** At fixed effective contexts (test 8K/16K/32K only where memfit permits), require all final requests fit with reserved output. Bound retrieval candidates (initial experiment cap 32), retrieved source/brief selections (e.g. 8 each), one retrieval round per turn and history/UI caches (proposed cap 64MiB), then vary those caps explicitly if quality needs more. Start summary work as one bounded batch (~2K source tokens) and cancel/preempt for chat; measure p95/p99 foreground input-to-paint, request preparation, TTFT and token gaps plus memory pressure/swap/whole-system usage. Candidate UI goals from MEMORY-001 are p95 ≤50ms, p99 ≤100ms, **not accepted SLAs**; propose no >10% foreground latency regression against the matched baseline and no sustained memory-pressure/swap growth at the representative workload. Establish actual available summary/sidecar RAM headroom with MariaDB, Qdrant, editor and embedder running—do not infer it from “16GB” or process RSS. No extra 8B embedder/4B reranker is an acceptable hidden prerequisite. [MEMORY-001](../../docs/MEMORY-001-long-lived-context.md); [MODEL-001](../../docs/MODEL-001-lfm3-runtime.md)
6. **Authority/privacy zero-tolerance tests.** Wrong-project UUID, local-only history followed by explicit remote switch, source prompt injection and special tokens, private observer finding without Discuss, draft hypothesis versus committed canon, deletion/retraction mid-summary, attachment-scope removal, stale job, tampered derivative, Qdrant wipe/down, MariaDB down, socket auth failure, cancellation, crash/restart and offline operation. Verify no auto-remote fallback, no unauthorized network egress, no hidden edit/canon promotion, and no data crossing prohibited scope even through summaries. Derived eligibility is the intersection of underlying-source permissions; inability to retrieve must surface as unavailable/partial, not “nothing was said.” [SOW](../../docs/SOW.md); [chat remote filtering](../../src/llm/chat.c); [local socket/injection constraints](../../docs/MODEL-001-lfm3-runtime.md)
7. **Decision rule.** Choose the simplest arm with a meaningful paired improvement over A/B on the agreed continuity tasks, no unacceptable exact-detail/abstention regressions, and all privacy/authority/resource gates passed. Publish per-task errors and overhead, multiple seeds, confidence intervals/paired comparisons and an hours-long soak/restart run. If C does not outperform B, retain retrieval-only; if D adds drift/cost without benefit, do not add tiers; if LFM3 fails summarization/tool-use, do not silently offload to a remote or larger model. The proposal is falsifiable precisely because native/hierarchical memory is not presumed to win. [GAP-003](../../docs/PRODUCT-GAPS.md); [paper workload sensitivity][P]

### Gaps

**Material caveats the final report must not omit:**

- Billions are predominantly cumulative repeated input/cache reading, not a billion-token active window or billion unique archived facts; modeled 28.6B capacity is not an observed run. [P]
- The benchmark is v0.1.202, the source snapshot is package 0.1.212, and paper/pilot evidence spans older hosts/versions; no silent version substitution. [PKG][B][P]
- “Independent” is the hosted benchmark's description, not a replication established here. Raw predictions/harness/data required for full verification were not available in the inspected artifacts. [B][P]
- Good multi-session/coding results coexist with poor exact retrieval and weaker recommendation/book-summary results. Survival means no context overflow, not perfect recall. [B]
- Text retention ratios and pointer lineage do not prove semantic fidelity, spontaneous restoration or unchanged quality after repeated tiers. [P]
- Small-window doctrine overhead and zero spontaneous restoration even at 27B are direct warnings against assuming success for LFM3-1.7B; cache economics and large-model RAG results do not transfer automatically to the local BF16 runtime. [P][B]; [MODEL-001](../../docs/MODEL-001-lfm3-runtime.md)
- License text is MIT **plus additional visible-attribution terms**, and provenance is author-reported rather than independently audited. This is not legal advice. [L][KL][PROV]
- Long-lived memory does not bound history loading/UI/DB work or authorize broader data access. MariaDB/source authority, private socket, no automatic remote fallback, author approval and Discuss-only observer context remain hard boundaries. All experimental architecture/resource numbers above are proposals, not measured fit or authorization to implement. [SOW](../../docs/SOW.md); [PRODUCT-GAPS](../../docs/PRODUCT-GAPS.md)
